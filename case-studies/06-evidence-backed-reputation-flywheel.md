# 06 — Evidence-backed reputation & review intelligence

> First-party material. Virtual-team role names are **disclosed AI functions**, not human employees. Campaign artifacts default to **review-only** — nothing publishes externally without Keyholder approval.

## Buyer problem
Online reputation work often collapses into vanity metrics, ghostwritten reviews, or AI copy that cannot be audited. Buyers (systems engineers, agency owners, brand operators) need a **governed** way to turn real engineering proof into public claims — with counterevidence, confidence scores, and human gates.

## What ArchE demonstrates
1. **Claim ledger** — every public-facing statement resolves to repo paths, validation timestamps, confidence, and mandatory counterevidence.
2. **Disclosed virtual team** — Researcher, Proof Engineer, Reputation Auditor, and related roles are machine-readable AI contracts, not fictional staff.
3. **Episode → campaign packet** — one engineering episode can generate case study, research note, short posts, video storyboard, demo brief, offer, and distribution plan — all `queued_for_keyholder_review`.
4. **Publication adapter** — render (MP4, Markdown) is separated from irreversible publish; approval token + handoff required.
5. **Interactive review models** — portfolio demos show review velocity, detractor SLAs, and margin-vs-review-risk tradeoffs on scrubbed demo data (`reviews.demo` labels only).

## Sample outcome framing (not a live client result)
- Detect stale automation methods while preserving the original business goal and approval boundary.
- Refuse “green transport status” as proof that a workflow actually answered the buyer.
- Continue review-safe internal work between chat sessions via heartbeat — with failure retention.

## Limitations

- External review platforms (Google, Yelp, etc.) are not auto-posted by this engine.
