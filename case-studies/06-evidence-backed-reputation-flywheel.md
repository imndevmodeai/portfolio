1. **Agents assigned** — Experience Capture, Social Media Agent, YouTube Harvest, Review Desk, Auditor.

3. **Social Media Agent** — positive experience → brand drafts.

5. **Scoreboard** — moments → asks → stars → content → presence.
6. **SERP evidence** — BEFORE/AFTER Google screens as proof of stake and planned trajectory.
