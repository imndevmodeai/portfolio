## Key Capabilities (What It Does)

- **LLM orchestration:** Multiple providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, Mistral, local Ollama), fallbacks, and structured output.
- **Research & synthesis:** Multi-source web/search, scholarly-style retrieval, vetting, and synthesis into reports.
- **Causal inference:** Time-series and observational data; lagged effects and causal graph discovery.
- **Agent-based modeling (ABM):** Simulate emergent behavior from agent rules; used for scenario exploration.
- **Comparative Fluxual Processing (CFP):** Quantum-inspired comparison of system dynamics and trajectories.
- **Predictive modeling:** Forecasting and trend analysis with confidence intervals.
- **Knowledge layer:** Sparse Priming Representations (SPRs), symbol codex, and a persistent knowledge graph. Every action produces Integrated Action Reflection (IAR) for self-assessment and debugging.
- **Workflow engine:** DAG-based execution with conditions, phase gates, and IAR-driven branching.
- **Semantic compression (Zepto):** High-ratio compression of context for LLMs (300:1+ in testing).
- **Browser automation:** Headless and interactive flows for scraping and UI automation when needed.
