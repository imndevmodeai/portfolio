# Governed Agent Pods — pricing narrative

**Positioning:** Governed Multi-Agent Systems — teams of agents that finish complex work with audit trails, confidence scoring, and a human on the final Publish button.

## Ladder (primary)

1. **Diagnostic / Architecture workshop — $3,000–$8,000**  
   Process map → Pod selection → risk gates → scoreboard baseline → review-gated offer pack.
2. **Pilot Pod (30–60 days) — $8,000–$25,000**  
   One production Pod (Sales/Outreach, Research, CS/Support, Knowledge/Ops, or Reputation & Proof) with auth gates and audit pack.
3. **Production orchestration retainer — monthly**  
   Run, improve, and export audit/scoreboard packs. Scoped after pilot.

## Cash-path Reputation Pod (optional)

See [`reputation-flywheel-pricing.md`](reputation-flywheel-pricing.md):

- Diagnostic / setup often **$2,500–$7,500**
- Monthly flywheel priced where one incremental booked job or Maps recovery can exceed the fee

- Not a guarantee of rankings, revenue, or enterprise procurement outcomes.
- Demo numbers are scrubbed illustrations.
- External posts / paid actions require human approval.
