# SKU — Experience Capture Agent

> First-party offer sheet. Disclosed AI function · human Publish gate · never invents star ratings.

## One-liner
**Creates authentic Google stars without inventing them** — by catching praise that already happened at the job and turning it into a review ask the customer can post.

## What the customer gets
| Deliverable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Happy-moment detection | Signals when a job likely earned goodwill |
| Review-ask queue | Draft asks ready for your approval / send |
| Star attribution | Scoreboard line: moments → asks → authentic stars |
| Auditor gate | Fake / paid / fabricated stars blocked |

## What we refuse
- Invented reviews
- Buying stars
- Auto-posting to Google without your Publish

## Fits inside
Reputation flywheel (Scenario R) · can also stand alone for shops that only need review volume.

Diagnostic / setup often **$2,500–$7,500**. Ongoing capture priced so **one incremental booked job** from better Maps trust can exceed the monthly fee. Not a ranking or revenue guarantee.
